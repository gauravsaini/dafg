# Boeing 747 Three.js Simulation Goal

Build a high-fidelity, interactive 3D simulation of a Boeing 747-400 commercial aircraft using Three.js.
The simulation must support:
- Full structural aircraft geometry (Fuselage, Upper deck hump, Swept wings, 4 Turbofans with 24 blades, Tail, Multi-bogey landing gear)
- PBR materials and procedural canvas textures (Fuselage, Wings, Tail livery, Polished aluminum, Cockpit glass)
- Multi-angle camera inspection system with 10 presets
- Realistic scene lighting (Day, Sunset, Night) and airport runway environment
- Automated screenshot capture for all inspection angles
